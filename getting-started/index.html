    📘 Start here.¶

    Your cluster, your science. Four nodes, 512 cores, two V100 GPUs and 386 TB of shared storage are one account away. This path takes you from zero to your first job in about 30 minutes.

    Your path to the first job¶

    • Step 0 — Get an account

    Ask the lab administrator for an HPC account. You will receive a username, a temporary password and SSH access to master.

    • Step 1 — Connect

    Log in from your laptop with SSH (or a terminal on Linux/macOS).

    What you need before starting¶

    • an HPC account (username + password)
    • an SSH client — terminal on macOS/Linux, Windows Terminal or PowerSSH/PuTTY on Windows
    • basic Linux comfort (cd, ls, mkdir) — the guides explain the rest

    The one rule, repeated

    Anything on scratch (/nvme*, node-local /DATA) can be deleted at any time. The only copy that survives is the one on shared NFS storage.
